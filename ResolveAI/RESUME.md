# Resume points — use once the project is implemented and tested

**ResolveAI — AI-Powered Customer Support Platform** | React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Gemini API, JWT

- Built **RESTful APIs** for customer support ticket management with **JWT authentication** and **role-based authorization** for customers, agents and administrators.
- Integrated **Gemini API** for on-demand complaint classification, sentiment detection, priority recommendations and AI-generated reply drafts with human approval.
- Designed a relational **PostgreSQL** schema with **Prisma ORM** for users, tickets, messages and agent assignments, incorporating transactional updates and audit logs.
- Developed a responsive **React + TypeScript dashboard** for ticket tracking, team assignment, conversation history and support analytics.
